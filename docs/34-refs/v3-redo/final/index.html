Baaz v3: the four screens

Fluid Functionalism's inset surfaces: the sidebar lives on the base (s1 #171717); the work area is a card one step up (s2 #1E1E1E, border/60 edge, shadow-2, radius 10, 8 px from the window on every side). Closed, the card stays and two pills float over it: lights, Show sidebar, the mode switch and the title on the left, the actions on the right (s3, shadow-3, 36 px, fully round). Content scrolls under the pills through a fade. Composed from captures of the built app; each image opens at full size.

Sessions · open

The sidebar carries the window row. The session title row sits inside the card; the transcript scrolls under it. The composer lifts one more step inside the card (s3, shadow-3).

Sessions · closed

The card fills the window. Left pill: lights · Show sidebar · Sessions/Browse · title. Right pill: terminal · menu · right pane. The transcript re-centres and runs under the pills.

Browse · open

The same card holds the page; address, controls and tabs live in the sidebar, as built.

Browse · closed

Left pill: lights · Show sidebar · switch · back · forward · reload · title and host at rest. Right pill: Ask · page menu · new tab. No tab strip; tabs are one hover away in the peek, or ⌘1–9. The pills are overlay windows above the native page.
