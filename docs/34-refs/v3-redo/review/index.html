Baaz v3 surfaces: the decisions, with the screens

Every screen below is composed from captures of the built app (test bundle, this evening). Only the thing being decided is redrawn. The previous v3 draft was invented from scratch; this one changes the real screen and nothing else. Reply with a decision per item, or "all as recommended".

D0The frame: what is a card?

This is the decision the other nine hang off. The draft made the session itself a card. Claude Desktop, the reference you pointed at, does the opposite: the conversation lives on the base and only the things beside it are cards. The page in Browse must be a card either way (a foreign white rectangle needs a rounded inset).

U1 · everything is a cardSidebar on the base, the session in an s2 card, composer lifted to s3 inside it. FF's inset sidebar, literally. Reads boxed: a frame around what you read, and the composer floats inside a float.
U2 · the base is the roomSidebar and transcript share the base, no line between them. Cards are the composer, the right pane, the page, the peek sidebar, menus, dialogs. The title row sits on the base and the transcript scrolls under it through a 48 px fade. This is Claude Desktop's model on FF's values.

Recommend U2. The rule becomes one sentence: you read and write on the base; anything you look at or reach for is a card lifted one step. Sessions and Browse then share one look (compare the Browse boards under D2), and nothing floats inside a float.

D1Edge strength on a card

FF's dark shadow-2 ring is 2 % white and reads as nothing on a 7-unit fill step. Claude Desktop draws a real 1 px line (measured #303030 on a #1D1D1D card). FF has two line tokens of its own: border (foreground 12 %) and border/60 (7.2 %), which it uses for menu separators. Crops at 2×, composer corner and right-pane edge:

A · FF exactshadow-2 alone: the 2 % ring. The card is a tone, not an object.
B · border/60 + shadow-2FF's separator token on the card edge. Lands within a few units of Claude Desktop's line, and it is still an FF value.
C · border + shadow-2The full 12 % line (what the build uses for every hairline today). Crisper, but every card starts to look like a form field.

Recommend B. Fill step + border/60 + shadow-2 for every s2 card on the base. Popups and dialogs keep their own recipes (shadow-3, shadow-5), which already carry a black 12–16 % outer ring.

You asked for the header bar to go, with "only the traffic lights plus a few icons / the title in a fixed pill-like surface". I built the pill properly and then measured Claude Desktop collapsed: it has no pill. The lights, toggle, mode switch and title sit on the base with no fill and no line, and the content scrolls underneath. Top-left at 2×:

Reference · Claude Desktop collapsedLights, sidebar toggle, chat/code switch, back/forward, title. Nothing behind them.

P · pills, refined32 px, s2 + border/60, lights inside, regular-weight title, actions in a second pill. Better than the draft, still two floating objects over text.

Browse is where this is decided, because pills over the page would have to be overlay windows covering the site's own top-left. With N, the row simply stays on the base and the page card keeps its 8 px inset below it; the card does not change shape when the sidebar goes.

Browse openAs built, with the D1 edge on the page card.
Browse closed · NOne row: lights · Show sidebar · switch · back/forward/reload · title and host at rest · Ask · ⋯ · +. No tab strip (see D9). The page card below, 8 px inset.

Recommend N for both modes. It is the reference's own answer, it needs no overlay windows, the lights never move, and Sessions and Browse collapse into the same row. P stays on file if you want the pill anyway.

D3Traffic lights

Under N they never move. Under P they would jump into the pill at the end of the close and back at the start of the open (per-frame stepping is untested). Moot if D2 = N.

D4Right pane level

Right pane as an s2 card (8 px inset, D1 edge), tabs inside its header. The model picker is open from the composer: a popup lifts two steps from its substrate, so from the s2 composer it lands at s4 with the shadow pinned at 3.

Recommend the sibling card at s2. Claude Desktop's panel is darker and cooler than its base; FF's ladder only lifts, and a darker panel beside a lifted composer would put two depth systems on one screen.

D5Lines

Rule: no line ever separates two surfaces; a line may run inside one surface. That removes the session header hairline (the fade replaces it) and the composer's top rule (it is a card now). Two remain in question:

Sidebar hairline keptFF's variant="sidebar" and Claude Desktop both keep a faint inner-edge line.
No sidebar hairlineSidebar and transcript are one surface; the transcript column starts 150 px further in anyway. In Browse the card edge is the divider, so this makes the two modes identical.

Recommend no sidebar hairline; keep the footer rule (it is inside the sidebar, FF's own separator). Same for separators inside menus and the diff.

D6Row contents when collapsed

  • Sessions: title only, semibold, as today. The project · branch crumb returns only when two projects are open at once (as the build already does); otherwise it is noise next to the title.
  • Browse: back · forward · reload · the address at rest (shield, title semibold, host muted) · Ask · ⋯ · +. ⌘L still opens the Field over the address.
  • Mode switch: stays in the row when collapsed, parked after the toggle (Claude Desktop keeps its chat/code switch there too). ⌥⌘B works either way.

Recommend as listed.

D7Collapsed card inset

8 px all round, unchanged from open. The Browse card never changes shape; only the sidebar column leaves. Full bleed would make the collapse a second layout rather than the same layout minus one column.

D8Composer

One card, chips inside, as built. s2 + the D1 edge, radius 10, 824 wide, 12 px above the window edge, no top rule. Claude Desktop splits input from an options row because its options are a separate control row; ours are chips that belong to the message. Inside a narrower centre (right pane open) it shrinks to the column minus 48.

D9Carried over from docs/33

  • (a) Switch parked after the toggle when closed: yes (D6).
  • (b) Browse closed tab strip: no strip. Closed means the page; tabs are one hover away in the peek, plus ⌘1–9 and the Field.

Depth on a real screenDialog at s5 + shadow-5 over a 40 % scrim; its select opens at s7; tooltip inverted, no shadow; toast = popup rules (s3, shadow-3) bottom right. Buttons: hint left, secondary, primary right.
PeekThe sidebar as a card: s2, D1 edge, shadow-5, radius 10, under the chrome row (so the lights stay where they are), 8 px from the left and bottom. Slides in on spring.moderate, out on its exit tween.
