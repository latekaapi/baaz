# UI audit — 2026-09-13 (owner round 3)

Every surface the app can be driven to without spending a turn, read as a
senior product designer would read it: alignment and rhythm, hierarchy,
truncation and overflow, states, consistency, copy, theme parity, motion.

**Method.** `scripts/captures.sh` (60 PNGs: every replay fixture in both
themes, the login states, the Projects surfaces) and `scripts/journeys.sh`
(the J1 journeys, each a `--steps` script with its own capture), both under
`HARNESS_DETERMINISTIC=1` and both run twice with the pair compared, so a
finding is never an artefact of one frame. Every capture cited here was read;
none of the findings is inferred from source alone.

**Ranking.** **P1** wrong or broken · **P2** visibly off · **P3** polish.
P1 and P2 are fixed in this round unless the entry says otherwise and says
why. P3 is the numbered follow-up list in §9.

**What this audit could not reach.** Three classes, all noted where they
bite rather than silently skipped:

- **Hover and pressed states.** There is no way to move the pointer from a
  script on this machine (no `cliclick`, no Quartz binding), and a capture
  renders wherever the pointer happens to be. Hover trays, hover grounds and
  pressed states are therefore reviewed from source and from the gallery's
  at-rest states only — with one piece of luck worth recording, because it is
  the round's only hover evidence: in the `sidebar-wide` journey the pointer
  happened to be resting on the `acme-internal` project row, so that capture
  shows **D6 working** — the row's tray (`+` and `…`) is up and its count is
  gone, while `acme-web` below it, unhovered, still carries its `7`. Luck is
  not a method: a `hover:<selector>` step verb would make this testable.
- **Anything behind a live turn.** Streaming text, the running status row and
  a real approval need a billed turn. The free live route through
  `session/userShell` is closed on this machine: Muse answers *"managed shell
  sandbox is unavailable"* (§8, finding 12).
- **Motion.** A still cannot show a transition. Motion findings come from the
  code and from comparing captures taken at 4 s, 9 s and 14 s.

## 1. Transcript — tool cards

Captures: `synthetic-toolshapes-{dark,light}.png`,
`synthetic-toolgroup-{dark,light}.png`, `transcript-1.2.1-shapes-*.png`.

**P1 · A tool the app does not recognise is titled with its arguments.**
`synthetic-toolshapes` calls `estimate` with
`{"task": "tidy", "depth": 2}`, and the card's title read
`Ran {"task": "tidy", "depth": 2}` — a JSON dump where the tool's name
belongs. The wire carries `"tool": "estimate"`; `tool_shape`'s fallback arm
looked for a `command` or a `path` field, found neither, and fell back to the
raw argument string, discarding the one word that says what happened. The
arguments are already drawn in the card's body as parameter pairs (`task
tidy`, `depth 2`), so the title had nothing to gain from repeating them.
**Fixed** — the last resort is the tool's own name, so the card reads
`Ran estimate`. One line of snapshot moved
(`synthetic-toolshapes.json`: `"target": "estimate"`).

**P2 · Three tool cards, three different amounts of trailing information.**
In one capture: `Ran ls -la` carries `exit 0 · 0.1 s`, `Read …/notes.txt`
carries `3 lines`, and `Ran {…}` carries nothing at all — same card family,
same row, no rule a reader can learn. A card that has run either has a
duration or it does not; the duration should not depend on which family the
tool fell into. **Not fixed this round** — the cells come from three separate
presentation arms in `tool_presentation`, and making them agree is a
behaviour decision about what a card promises (is a missing duration
"instant" or "unknown"?), not a defect with one right answer. P3-1.

**P3 · The code block header says the word "code".** A fenced Rust block's
header reads `code rust` beside a file glyph. "code" is the block's own kind
restated; the language alone is the information. P3-2.

**P3 · "Ran" covers three unrelated things** — a shell command, an MCP tool
call and an unrecognised tool. "Read", "Wrote", "Edited" and "Searched" are
specific; "Ran" is the bucket. P3-3.

## 2. Transcript — turn footer

Captures: `synthetic-markdown-{dark,light}.png`, and every transcript capture.

**P2 · "0 tokens" is drawn when the count is unknown.** A replayed turn's
footer reads `muse-spark-1.3 · 2.3 s · 0 tokens`. No turn that produced that
much output cost zero tokens; zero here means *the wire did not say*, and the
footer states it as a fact. The same footer already drops the cost cell when
it is zero (`if meta.cost_usd > 0.0`), so the precedent for dropping an
unknown cell is in the same function. **Fixed** — the tokens cell is dropped
at zero, in the library's `footer_items` and in the harness's
`silent_footer_items` that mirrors it.

**P2 · The per-turn action row is very low contrast in both themes.** The
copy / retry / fork glyphs under a turn sit at `ink_4` on the transcript
ground; at 12 px they are close to invisible in light and only a little
better in dark. They are the only way to copy a turn with the pointer.
**Not fixed this round** — the row is at idle opacity by an owner decision
taken on 2026-09-10 (list item C6, "the in-flow action row under each turn at
idle opacity, although the design's toolbar is hover-only"), so raising it is
a decision to re-take with the owner rather than a defect to correct. P3-4.

## 3. Sidebar

Captures: `projects-sidebar-{dark,light}.png`, the journeys' `sidebar-*`,
`group-by-*`, and the library gallery's `sidebar/views` at 240 and 520 px.

**P1 · Rows under a project did not share the project row's right edge.**
Fixed in the library half of this round (D2) — see the changelog. At 520 px
the rows stopped ~250 px short of the project row; at 240 px their ground ran
past its right margin. Both are gone, and the gallery now carries both widths
as states so the edge is checkable without the app.

**P2 · A project with no sessions shows a count of `0`.** `harness 0`,
`upsc-hybrid-v1 0`. The count is there to say how much is inside; at zero it
says only that the row is empty, which the absent rows already say, and it
puts a meaningless digit on the same baseline as the meaningful ones.
**Fixed** — the count is dropped at zero.

**P3 · A wide sidebar leaves the session rows' middle empty.** At 420 px a
row is `title … time` with the title's natural width on the left and the time
at the right edge, so the two drift apart with nothing between them. The row
reads as two columns rather than one line. P3-5.

## 4. Sidebar footer

Capture: `d4d5-dark.png` (scratchpad), `projects-sidebar-*.png`.

**P2 · The plan was printed twice.** The label read
`Power Usage · 38% weekly` and the meter under it drew 38% again. Fixed this
round (D5): the label is the plan's name, the meter row carries the number.

**P3 · The plan label floats far from the name it qualifies.** The row reads
`Replay      · High Usage` with roughly 115 px of nothing between them,
because the name cell holds `FOOTER_NAME_MIN` whatever the name's own width
is, and the middot travels with the plan rather than the name. Not wrong —
the separator and the order are both right — but the two read as unrelated.
Letting the name shrink-wrap would close it; the comments around
`plan_drop` / `FOOTER_NAME_MIN` describe a carefully-tuned yield order
between name and plan, so this wants a deliberate change rather than a
one-line one. P3-11.

*(An earlier draft of this audit recorded a "leading separator" fault here.
That was wrong: the middot is only ever drawn with the name present, so it
never leads. The gap is the finding.)*

## 6. Empty and error states

Captures: `synthetic-empty-*.png`, `synthetic-error-retry-*.png`,
`synthetic-wirefault-*.png`, `synthetic-modelrouteunserved-*.png`,
`synthetic-turn-removed-*.png`.

**P2 · A tool failure prints the wire's sentence verbatim, and it overflows.**
The live run in §8 showed
`tool failed: environment failure: managed shell sandbox is unavailable` and,
under it, `The execution environment is broken: the command was never started
and every later command will fail the same way` — one unwrapped line running
into the card's right edge with its last word clipped mid-glyph (`…the same
wa▌`), and more sentence after it that the reader can never reach.

**Not fixed this round**, and the reason matters. The text is drawn by
`shell_body`, which is `whitespace_nowrap` + `overflow_hidden` **on purpose**:
command output is column-aligned and must not reflow. The fault is not that
`shell_body` does not wrap — it is that a *failure sentence*, which is prose,
is being routed into the body meant for terminal output. The honest fixes are
either to give a tool failure its own body (an adapter change to
`tool_presentation`) or to let the shell body scroll horizontally as a real
terminal does (`overflow_x_scroll`, one container around all lines, which
changes every tool card in the app and every capture of one). Both are
larger and riskier than this round should start on a finding discovered in
its last hours; neither should be guessed at. P3-7, with the reproduction
above.

## 7. Theme parity

Every fixture was captured in both themes and the pairs compared. Structure,
measure, row metrics and wrapping are identical in all 27 pairs — no layout
differs between the themes. The findings above that mention contrast (§2) are
present in both, not in one.

## 8. What a live session showed

One free live run (no turn: `session/start` + `session/userShell`, both on the
free list) against `muse 1.2.1`:

**P2 · finding 12 — the tier banner contradicts the tier probe.** The footer
raised *"Muse did not say which plan this login is on, so the harness cannot
tell whether turns bill API usage"* with a **Check again** action, in a run
begun a minute after `--print-tier` printed `Subscription: Muse Code Power
Usage · Weekly: 38% used` from the same login. Whatever the in-app probe does
differs from what `--print-tier` does. **Not fixed this round** — it is a
wire-timing question (the probe has a deadline; the banner is what it shows
when the deadline passes) and diagnosing it properly needs a live session to
watch, which this round's spend budget is reserved for elsewhere. P3-8, with
the reproduction recorded here.

**Environmental, not a defect:** `session/userShell` cannot run on this
machine at all — *"managed shell sandbox is unavailable … every later command
will fail the same way"*. This is what closed the free route to a live
approval (D1), and it is worth knowing before the next round plans around
`shell:`.

## 9. P3 follow-ups

1. Tool cards' trailing cells do not agree (duration / result / nothing);
   decide what a card promises, then make all three arms of
   `tool_presentation` keep that promise. §1
2. A fenced block's header says "code" as well as its language. §1
3. "Ran" is the bucket verb for a shell command, an MCP call and an unknown
   tool; three different things reading the same. §1
4. The per-turn action row is barely visible at idle opacity. Re-take the
   2026-09-10 C6 decision with the owner: hover-only as the design has it, or
   a step up in contrast. §2
5. A session row at a wide sidebar reads as two columns with a gap; consider
   a max measure for the row's content, as the transcript has. §3
6. `captures.sh` takes the five login states in dark only. §5
7. A tool *failure* is rendered in the body meant for terminal output, so it
   is clipped instead of wrapped and the reader cannot reach the rest of the
   sentence. Give a failure its own body, or let the shell body scroll
   sideways. The copy being the server's verbatim is the second half of the
   same finding. §6
8. The in-app tier probe and `--print-tier` disagree about the same login. §8
9. `HARNESS_DETERMINISTIC=1` freezes the clock per *process*, not to a fixed
   instant, so two runs of the same capture taken minutes apart disagree
   wherever a relative label ("4h", "now") crosses a boundary — 10 of 69
   capture pairs and 11 of 55 journeys, every one of them a label and nothing
   else. A `HARNESS_NOW=<rfc3339>` would make the suite reproducible across
   invocations and retire the sub-pixel-AA caveat carried since 2026-09-12.
   See `journeys-2026-09-13.md` §3. A few lines in `crate::clock`.
10. `list()`'s 72 px uniform row hint (`ROW_HEIGHT_HINT`) is re-based into a
   real height as each row is measured, which makes the logical-to-pixel
   mapping move under a gesture. Not what the owner was seeing — the burst
   measurement in `docs/diagnosis/scroll-research-2026-09-13.md` §2a settled
   that — but it is a real second-order source of uneven travel, and
   per-block-kind hints would remove it.
11. The sidebar footer’s plan label sits ~115 px from the name it qualifies,
    because the name cell holds its minimum width. §4
12. The `y` / `a` / `n` approval actions are bound by the library
    (`aui/src/keys.rs`) and handled by nobody; the card draws `1`–`N` and the
    harness handles `ChooseNth`. Either handle the triad or stop binding it —
    as it stands three keys are advertised in the library's own keymap table
    and do nothing.
